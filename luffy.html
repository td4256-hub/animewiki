Monkey D. Luffy

One Piece

About Luffy

Monkey D. Luffy is the main protagonist of One Piece. He dreams of becoming the Pirate King.

Luffy travels across the seas with his Straw Hat Pirates crew in search of the legendary One Piece treasure.

Personality

Luffy is cheerful, adventurous, determined, and loyal to his friends. He always fights to protect the people he cares about.

Powers and Abilities

  • Devil Fruit abilities
  • Observation Haki
  • Armament Haki
  • Conqueror's Haki
  • Gear transformations

Relationships

Luffy is the captain of the Straw Hat Pirates. His close crewmates include Zoro, Nami, Usopp, and Sanji.

Dream

Luffy's greatest dream is to become the Pirate King and achieve freedom on the seas.
