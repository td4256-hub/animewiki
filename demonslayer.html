DEMON SLAYER: KIMETSU NO YAIBA

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba follows Tanjiro Kamado, a young boy whose family is attacked by demons. His younger sister, Nezuko, survives but is transformed into a demon.

Tanjiro joins the Demon Slayer Corps in an attempt to find a way to turn Nezuko back into a human while fighting demons and protecting other people.

Plot

Beginning

Tanjiro lives with his family and earns money by selling charcoal. After returning home one day, he discovers that his family has been attacked by a demon.

Nezuko is the only member of his immediate family to survive, but she has become a demon. Tanjiro begins searching for a way to restore her humanity.

Demon Slayer Corps

Tanjiro eventually joins the Demon Slayer Corps, an organization dedicated to fighting demons. He trains in swordsmanship and develops his abilities while taking part in missions against demons.

Tanjiro uses Water Breathing and later develops the Hinokami Kagura technique. The official Demon Slayer site identifies these techniques as part of his fighting abilities.

Characters

Tanjiro Kamado is the main protagonist. He is kind-hearted and joins the Demon Slayer Corps to find a way to turn Nezuko back into a human.

Nezuko Kamado is Tanjiro's younger sister. After being turned into a demon, she continues to protect humans.

Zenitsu Agatsuma is a Demon Slayer Corps swordsman who uses Thunder Breathing.

Inosuke Hashibira is a Demon Slayer Corps swordsman who uses Beast Breathing and wears a boar mask.

Setting

Demon Slayer is set in a historical Japan-inspired world where demons exist alongside humans. The Demon Slayer Corps operates to protect people from these demons.

Conclusion

Demon Slayer combines sword-based action, supernatural elements, family, perseverance, and the relationship between Tanjiro and Nezuko.
