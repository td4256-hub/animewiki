Eren Yeager

Attack on Titan

About Eren Yeager

Eren Yeager is one of the central characters in Attack on Titan. He dreams of exploring the world beyond the walls.

After his hometown is attacked by Titans, Eren decides to join the military and fight against the Titans.

Personality

Eren is determined, passionate, and strongly believes in freedom. His personality changes throughout the story.

Powers and Abilities

  • Attack Titan
  • Founding Titan
  • War Hammer Titan

Relationships

Eren is closely connected to Mikasa Ackerman and Armin Arlert, his childhood friends.
