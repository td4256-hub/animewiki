Tanjiro Kamado

Demon Slayer

About Demon Slayer

Tanjiro Kamado is the central character in Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba. He is a kind-hearted boy who wants to protect his family and find a way to turn his sister Nezuko back into a human.

After returning home one day to discover that his family has been attacked by a demon, Tanjiro decides to become a Demon Slayer and fight against demons.

Personality

Tanjiro is kind, compassionate, determined, and courageous. He cares deeply about his friends and family and often shows compassion even toward his enemies.

Powers and Abilities

  • Water Breathing
  • Hinokami Kagura
  • Enhanced swordsmanship

Relationships

Tanjiro is closely connected to his younger sister Nezuko Kamado. He also becomes close friends with Zenitsu Agatsuma and Inosuke Hashibira during his journey as a Demon Slayer.
