Overview

Attack on Titan is a Japanese anime series adapted from the manga by Hajime Isayama. The story takes place in a world where humanity lives behind enormous walls to protect itself from giant humanoid creatures known as Titans.

The series follows Eren Yeager, Mikasa Ackerman, and Armin Arlert as they face the dangers outside the walls and uncover the secrets surrounding their world.

Plot

Fall of Wall Maria

The story begins in the district of Shiganshina, where Eren Yeager lives with his family and friends. Humanity has lived behind massive walls for generations to protect itself from Titans.

One day, a Colossal Titan appears and breaks through the outer defenses. A powerful Armored Titan also destroys part of the wall, allowing other Titans to enter the city.

During the attack, Eren witnesses a tragedy that changes his life. He promises to eliminate every Titan and joins the military to fight against them.

Training and the Survey Corps

Eren, Mikasa, and Armin join the military and receive training to fight Titans. They learn to use vertical maneuvering equipment and discover the dangers of life outside the walls.

Eren later discovers that he possesses the ability to transform into a Titan. This discovery changes humanity's understanding of the conflict and makes him an important figure in the fight for survival.

The Secrets Behind the Walls

As the story progresses, the characters investigate the origins of the Titans, the history of the walls, and the mysteries surrounding their society.

Their journey reveals information about the world beyond the walls and the long history of conflict between different groups of people. The characters are forced to make difficult decisions about freedom, survival, and war.

The Final Conflict

The final part of the story explores the consequences of the characters' choices and the conflict between the people inside and outside the walls.

Eren, Mikasa, Armin, and their allies face a major conflict that determines the future of their world.

Main Characters

Eren Yeager

Eren Yeager is the main protagonist of Attack on Titan. He dreams of freedom beyond the walls and becomes a central figure in the conflict involving Titans and humanity.

Mikasa Ackerman

Mikasa Ackerman is a skilled soldier and one of Eren's closest companions. She is known for her exceptional combat abilities and her strong determination to protect the people she cares about.

Armin Arlert

Armin Arlert is an intelligent and thoughtful member of the group. His strategic thinking and knowledge play an important role in many missions.

Levi Ackerman

Ackerman is a highly skilled soldier of the Survey Corps. He is respected for his combat abilities, discipline, and leadership.

Erwin Smith

Erwin Smith is the former commander of the Survey Corps. He is known for his leadership, strategic planning, and determination to uncover the truth.

Reiner Braun

Reiner Braun is a warrior who plays a significant role in the conflict between Marley and the people of Paradis Island.

World of Attack on Titan

The world of Attack on Titan includes several important locations, including Paradis Island, Marley, and the walled territories where humans live.

The series explores the history of the Eldians, the Marleyan government, the Titans, and the conflicts that shaped the world.

Anime Adaptation

The Attack on Titan anime began airing in 2013. The first three seasons were produced by Wit Studio, while the final season was produced by MAPPA.

The anime concluded in 2023 with Attack on Titan: The Final Chapters.
