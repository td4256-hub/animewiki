Izuku Midoriya

My Hero Academia

About My Hero Academia

Izuku Midoriya is the central character in My Hero Academia. He is a determined and kind-hearted boy who dreams of becoming a professional hero and helping others.

Izuku was born without a Quirk in a world where superhuman abilities are common. After meeting All Might, he inherits the powerful Quirk One For All and begins his journey at U.A. High School.

Personality

Izuku is kind, courageous, determined, and compassionate. He cares deeply about helping others and often puts himself in danger to protect people who need help.

Powers and Abilities

  • One For All
  • Blackwhip
  • Danger Sense

Relationships

Izuku is closely connected to All Might, who becomes his mentor. He also has a complicated rivalry with his childhood acquaintance Katsuki Bakugo and becomes close friends with many of his classmates at U.A. High School.
